Hi, I'm Mark

I know what it's like to have all the answers — except the right ones

For years, I moved fast. New roles, new challenges, each one a little bigger than the last. I was good at it. I told myself the unease I felt was just ambition looking for its next outlet.

It took stepping back — really stepping back — to understand that I had been building someone else's version of success. The realization was uncomfortable. It was also the most useful thing that ever happened to me.

That experience is at the heart of how I coach. I'm not interested in quick fixes or frameworks that paper over complexity. I'm interested in helping you understand yourself clearly enough to make decisions you can actually stand behind.

I trained with the ICF-accredited Co-Active Training Institute and have worked with leaders, entrepreneurs, and professionals across industries navigating transitions, identity questions, and the quiet crises that don't show up on a CV.

— Mark

How we work together

No formulas. No prescriptions. Real conversation.

Coaching is thinking out loud with someone trained to ask the question that changes the frame. Here's roughly what that looks like.

First

A free introductory session

We spend an hour together at no cost. You bring whatever is most alive for you right now. I ask questions. You leave with at least one thing that's useful — and we both get a sense of whether working together makes sense.

Then

A focused engagement

Most clients work with me over three to six months, meeting every two weeks. Sessions are one hour, online or in person. Between sessions, there's space to apply what emerged. The pace is yours.

Throughout

Honest, rigorous presence

I'll say what I notice. I'll sit with you in the uncomfortable parts without rushing past them. And when the path forward becomes clear, we'll make sure it's yours — not the one that looks right on paper.
